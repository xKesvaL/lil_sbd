## What this course is for

This course is built for hard dance music producers first. The examples lean toward hardcore, uptempo, and zaag-heavy sound design, but the workflow carries over well to other EDM lanes.

The focus stays on production. The goal is to explain what I am doing, why I am doing it, and where the tradeoffs are when you want a track to hit hard without turning into shapeless noise.

## How to use it

Start with the chapter pages if you want the roadmap.

Go straight into the lesson pages if you already know the topic you need.

If a lesson is marked as coming soon, it means the topic is planned and the page exists on purpose, but the full walkthrough is not ready yet.

## What to expect

- Kick design from basic building blocks.
- Leads, melodies, and arrangement choices that keep aggression musical.
- Mixing and mastering decisions focused on control, not just loudness.
- Publishing and workflow material for getting a track over the line.

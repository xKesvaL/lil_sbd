## What this chapter is about

This chapter covers the melodic side without pretending that theory alone writes the track for you.

The focus is practical: build a few dependable lead types, understand the minimum theory that keeps you in control, and use references intelligently instead of freezing up because the melody did not arrive out of nowhere.

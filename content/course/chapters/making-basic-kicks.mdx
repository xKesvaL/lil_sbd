## Why kicks come first

If the kick is wrong, the track usually fights you for the rest of the session.

This chapter is about getting to a solid base quickly, then shaping it toward the style you actually want instead of endlessly resampling without a target.

## What is inside this chapter

- A gabber-oriented starting point.
- The jump toward hardcore weight.
- How rawstyle and uptempo priorities differ.
- Where zaag starts being useful and where it just starts eating the track.

## What this lesson will cover

This lesson will move from the gabber base into a harder and heavier hardcore balance.

The goal is not just to make the kick louder. The point is to decide where the extra weight should live, how much space the tail should take, and how far the distortion can go before it starts fighting the groove.

## Before it goes live

Make sure you are comfortable with the gabber lesson first. The hardcore lesson assumes you already know how to control the base shape and are ready to push it without losing definition.

French Uptempo DJ and producer. Zaag / Microwave kicks whilst keeping it music.

Based in Eastern France, Lil SBD brings relentless noise, as well as cross-genre twists to the scene. His sets tear through all types of hard dance music with one motto: hit hard, stay original.

From the east of France, Lil SBD approaches Uptempo in a different way: deliver crushing kicks without compromising on musicality or originality. He aims to collide multiple elements, such as catchy melodies, bold crossovers with other genres, or even unique themes. Inspired by the forward-thinking of acts such as EQUAL2, Albino and Lekkerfaces, Lil SBD makes sure no two DJ sets are ever the same, turning every experience into a one-of-a-kind. Driven by relentless energy and built to keep the crowd moving, he represents the new generation of uptempo, ready to leave a mark on line-ups and label rosters alike.
